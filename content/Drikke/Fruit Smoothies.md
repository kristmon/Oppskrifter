# Fruit Smoothies

## Ingredienser

- 2 fresh California peaches OR 4 fresh California plums
- 1 cup nonfat plain yogurt
- 1 cup lowfat milk
- several ice cubes OR crushed ice

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In blender or food processor, puree all ingredients until smooth.
2. Serve immediately.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 2
- **Type**: Beverage, Vegetarian
- **Kilde**: California Tree Fruit Agreement, www.eatcaliforniafruit.com
