# Grapefruit Sunriser

## Ingredienser

- 2 Texas Red Grapefruit
- 8 large strawberries (fresh or frozen)
- 8 oz strawberry-banana yogurt
- 2 ripe medium bananas
- 2 Tbs honey
- 1 cup crused ice

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Juice grapefruit; measure 1 1/3 cups juice. Add strawberries.
2. Cut bananas into chunks.
3. Combine all inredients in an electric blender and whirl until smooth

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4 Yield: Makes 4 (8 oz.) servings.
- **Type**: Beverage, Breakfast
- **Kilde**: Texas Sweet, www.texasweet.com
