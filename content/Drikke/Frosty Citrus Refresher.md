# Frosty Citrus Refresher

## Ingredienser

- 2 Texas Red Grapefruit
- 2 cups sliced fresh strawberries
- 1 cup pineapple sherbet
- 1 to 2 tablespoons honey
- Grapefruit wedges for garnish

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Juice grapefruit; measure 1 1/3 cups. In a blender, combine juice, strawberries, sherbet and 1 tablespoon honey.
2. Whirl until well-blended.
3. Sweeten with more honey, if desired.
4. Pour into glasses and garnish with grapefruit.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4 Yield: Makes 4 (8-ounce) servings.
- **Type**: Beverage
- **Kilde**: Texas Sweet, www.texasweet.com
