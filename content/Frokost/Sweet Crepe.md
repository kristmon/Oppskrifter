# Sweet Crepe

## Ingredienser

- 4 eggs
- 2 cups flour
- 2 1/2 cups milk
- 1/4 cup melted butter or oil
- dash salt
- 2 Tbs sugar

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Mix liquids first including melted butter or oil.
2. Add eggs.
3. Add flour last.
4. If wisking or blending by hand then mix for 3 minutes until smooth. If making in blender or food processor mix for 1 minute on high speed.
5. Stop and scrape down sides.
6. Blend smooth, 30 seconds to 1 minute
7. Use batter immediately if made in processor or blender, let rest for 1 hour if hand mixed. Use within 24 hours.
