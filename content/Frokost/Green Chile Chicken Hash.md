# Green Chile Chicken Hash

*Hash is the perfect dish for breakfast, lunch, or dinner and this recipe will put a new spin on the meal of your choice. Serve with eggs for breakfast and as a different side dish with your favorite lunch or dinner.*

## Ingredienser

- 3 Tbs butter
- 1 cup diced onion
- 1/4 cup chopped green bell pepper
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 4 cups diced cooked potatoes
- 1 (7-oz.) can Ortega® Diced Green Chiles
- 1 cup Ortega® Thick & Chunky Salsa
- 1 cup shredded cooked chicken
- 1/4 cup vegetable oil

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Melt butter in large skillet; add onion, bell pepper and chili powder. Cook for 5 minutes.
2. Add potatoes, chiles, salsa and chicken; cook for 2 minutes. Transfer mixture to medium bowl. Wipe out skillet; add oil. Return mixture to skillet; fry over medium heat for 10 minutes or until browned.
