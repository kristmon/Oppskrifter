# Texas Red Grapefruit Jelly

## Ingredienser

- 3 1/2 cups fresh squeezed Texas Red Grapefruit juice
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 7 cups sugar
- 2 (3 ounce) pouches liquid pectin
- red food coloring (optional)

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Combine all ingredients except the liquid pectin. Bring to a rolling boil for one minute.
2. Remove from heat and quickly skim off any foam.
3. Stir in the liquid pectin.
4. Add 6 to 8 drops red food coloring.
5. Ladle into hot sterilized jars and cover with lids and rings.
6. Process jars in boiling water for about 5 minutes to seal.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 16 Yield: 3 - 4 jars
- **Type**: Breakfast, Brunch, Side Dish
