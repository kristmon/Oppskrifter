# The Best Blueberry Muffins

## Ingredienser

- 2,5 dl fresh or frozen blueberries
- 1-3/10 dl plus 1 ss mel, divided
- 1,2 dl sukker
- 1 ts bakepulver
- 1/2 ts natron
- 1 ts muskatnøtt
- 3/4 ts salt
- 1 egg
- 2,5 dl surmelk
- 0,8 dl melk

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 400 F. Grease twelve 2-1/2-inch muffin cups.
2. Toss blueberries with 1 tablespoon of the mel. bland sammen the remaining 1-3/4 cup mel, the sukker, bakepulver, natron, muskatnøtt and salt; set aside.
3. Beat egg, surmelk and melk; rør into mel mixture til just combined (batter will be lumpy). rør in blueberries til evenly distributed. Fill muffin cups 2/3 full with batter. bak about 20 minutter til gyllen.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 12
- **Type**: Breakfast, Brunch
- **Kilde**: North American Blueberry Council, www.blueberry.org
