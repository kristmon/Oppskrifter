# Plum Cobbler

## Ingredienser

- 10 dl fresh, unpeeled plums
- 1,2 dl brunt sukker
**Topping**
- 2,5 dl mel
- 1,2 dl granulated sukker
- 1,2 dl brunt sukker
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/2 ts kanel
- 1 beaten egg
- 1,2 dl melted smør

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a 10"x10" casserole, cut up and bland plums with brunt sukker.
2. Topping: bland dry ingredients together, and tilsett beaten egg. bland will be crumbly.
3. Place over plums and drizzle with melted smør.
4. bak at 375º for 45 minutter.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 9
- **Type**: Dessert
