# Old-Fashioned Oatmeal Pie

## Ingredienser

- 1,9 dl honning
- 2 egg, beaten
- 1,9 dl quick cooking rolled oats
- 1,9 dl coconut
- 1,9 dl dried cranberries, currants or raisins
- 1,9 dl chopped walnuts
- 9-inch unbaked pie shell
- whipped fløte, if desired

## Fremgangsmåte

1. bland sammen all ingredients except pie shell and whipped fløte; bland well. hell into pie shell.
2. bak at 350°F 40 to 45 minutter eller til filling browns and knife blade inserted near center comes out clean.
3. Cool. Top with whipped fløte, if desired, and servér.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 8
- **Type**: Dessert
- **Kilde**: National Honey Board, www.honey.com
