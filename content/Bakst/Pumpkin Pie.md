# Pumpkin Pie

## Ingredienser

- 1 Can (16 ounces) solid pack pumpkin
- 1 Can (12 ounces) evaporated skim melk
- 2 egg
- 1,6 dl sukker
- 2 ts pumpkin pie spice
- 1 9-inch diameter pie shell unbaked

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In large bowl bland sammen pumpkin, melk, egg, sukker and pumpkin pie spice. hell into pie shell.
2. bak at 425 grader F. 15 minutter. Reduce ovn to 350 grader F. and bak 40 to 50 minutter eller til knife inserted in center comes out clean.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 12
- **Type**: Dessert, Pie, Thanksgiving
