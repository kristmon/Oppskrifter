# Thumbprint Cookies

## Ingredienser

- Nonstick cooking spray, as needed
- 3 Tbs Estee fructose
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp Estee Salt-It
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 cup unsalted margarine
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 Tbs orange fruit spread
- 1 Tbs water
- 1 egg white, slightly beaten
- 1/2 cup walnuts, finely chopped
- 4 Tbs Estee any-flavor fruit spread

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Lightly grease or spray cookie sheet with nonstick cooking spray. Mix together fructose, flour, Salt-It, and baking powder. Add margarine, vanilla extract, lemon juice, orange fruit spread, and water; mix to form a soft dough. Roll into 1-inch balls.
2. Roll each ball first in egg white, then in walnuts. Place on cookie sheet and press thumb into center of each. Bake 12 to 14 minutes; cool on wire rack. When cooled, fill each thumbprint with 1/4 teaspoon fruit spread.
