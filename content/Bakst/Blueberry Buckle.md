# Blueberry Buckle

*Makes 1 ten-inch round cake or 2 six-inch*

## Ingredienser

- 1,2 dl (1 stick), unsalted smør, room temperature, plus more for pan
- 5 dl all-purpose mel, plus more for pan
- 1 1/2 ts bakepulver
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1,9 dl sukker
- 1 large egg
- 1 ts pure vaniljeekstrakt
- 1,2 dl melk
- 5 cups wild or cultivated blueberries
**Streusel Topping (see tips)**

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Varm ovn to 350°. smør a springform baking pan, and dust with mel, tapping out excess. Set aside.
2. In a medium bowl, sift together the mel, bakepulver, and salt; set aside.
3. In the bowl of an electric mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, fløte smør and sukker on medium speed til light and fluffy, about 3 minutter. Reduce mixer speed to low, and tilsett egg and vanilje, beating til fully combined.
4. Tilsett reserved mel mixture, alternating with the melk, a little of each at a time, starting and ending with the mel mixture. Remove from mixer. forsiktig fold in blueberries.
5. hell batter into prepared pan; sprinkle streusel topping over cake. bak til cake tester comes out batter-free, 60 to 70 minutter. Transfer to a wire rack to cool for 10 minutter. Remove from pan; cool for 15 minutter before serving.
