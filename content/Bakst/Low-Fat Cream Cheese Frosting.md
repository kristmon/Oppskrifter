# Low-Fat Cream Cheese Frosting

## Ingredienser

- 1 1/3 cups plain nonfat yogurt, strained
- 3 cups skim-milk ricotta cheese
- 2 cups low-fat cottage cheese
- 1/3 cup fructose
- 3 Tbs evaporated skim milk

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Strain the yogurt overnight in cheesecloth over a bowl set in the refrigerator.
2. Combine all the ingredients in a large bowl; beat well with electric beaters until slightly stiff. Place in a covered container and refrigerate until ready to use (this frosting can be refrigerated for up to 1 week).
3. This recipe yields 48 servings. Serving size: 2 tablespoons.
4. Exchanges Per Serving: 1/2 Skim Milk.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 48
