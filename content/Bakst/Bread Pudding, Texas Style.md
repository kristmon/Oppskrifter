# Bread Pudding, Texas Style

## Ingredienser

**Cake**
- 1,2 dl smør or margarine, softened
- 1,9 dl sukker
- 4 egg
- 1 3/10 dl melk
- 1 1/2 ts kanel
- 1 1/2 ts muskatnøtt
- 1 1/2 ts vanilje
- 5 cups (1-inch) french bread cubes, crusts removed
- 0,8 dl raisins (optional)
**Citrus Pecan Sauce**
- 2 Texas Red Grapefruit, sectioned
- 2 Texas Oranges
- 0,6 dl sukker
- 1 1/2 ss cornstarch
- dash of salt
- 3 ss vann
- 2 ts smør or margarine
- 3 ss chopped pecans, toasted

## Fremgangsmåte

1. fløte smør and sukker til smooth. tilsett egg and beat well. rør in melk, kanel, muskatnøtt and vanilje.
2. Tilsett bread cubes and raisins, stirring just til bread is moistened.
3. hell into a greased 9-inch square baking pan. Let stand at room temperature for 15 minutter.
4. Cover and bak at 350 grader for 45 minutter.
5. Uncover and bak 10 minutter eller til knife inserted in center comes out clean.
6. servér warm or at room temperature with Citrus Pecan Sauce.
7. Peel and section one grapefruit and one orange over a bowl, reserving juice. Set sections aside.
8. Measure grapefruit juice; squeeze enough juice from remaining grapefruit to equal 6 tablespoons juice.
9. Measure orange juice; squeeze enough juice from remaining orange to equal 6 tablespoons juice. Set aside.
10. bland sammen sukker, cornstarch, and salt in a saucepan; rør in juices and vann. kok over medium varm, stirring konstant, til thickened and bubbly.
11. Tilsett smør and rør til melted. rør in grapefruit and orange sections and pecans.
12. servér warm or at room temperature with bread pudding.

## Info
