# Honey Vanilla Ice Cream

## Ingredienser

- 3 egg, well beaten
- 5 dl melk
- 1,2 dl honning
- 1/4 ts salt
- 5 dl half-and-half
- 1 ss vanilje

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Beat egg and melk together in large saucepan. rør in honning and salt. kok and rør over low varm about 10 minutter eller til thickened. Cool.
2. Rør in half-and-half and vanilje. Refrigerate til cold.
3. Freeze in ice fløte maker according to manufacturer's directions.
4. For a richer, sweeter ice fløte: Follow basic directions above but prepare egg-melk base with I cup each melk and half-and-half and 3/4 cup honning. tilsett 1 cup each half-and-half and whipping fløte to cooled mixture.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Dessert
- **Kilde**: National Honey Board, www.honey.com
