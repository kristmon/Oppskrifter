# Honey Chocolate Cupcakes

## Ingredienser

**Ingredients**
- 2,5 dl all-purpose mel
- 3 ss kakao
- 3/4 ts natron
- 1/4 ts salt
- 0,6 dl smør or margarine
- 1,9 dl honning
- 1 egg
- 1/2 ts vanilje
- 0,8 dl low fat melk
**sjokolade honning Glaze (recipe follows)**

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a small bowl, bland sammen mel, kakao, natron and salt; set aside.
2. Using electric mixer beat smør til light; gradually tilsett honning, beating til light and creamy. Beat in egg, vanilje and melk. Gradually tilsett mel mixture, mixing til well blended.
3. Spoon batter into 12 paper-lined or greased muffin cups, filling each 3/4 full. bak at 350°F for 20 to 25 minutter, eller til toothpick inserted in center comes out clean. Remove from ovn to wire rack. la avkjøle in pan for 5 minutter; remove from pan to wire rack to cool completely.
4. bred top of each cupcake with sjokolade honning Glaze, if desired.
5. In a small saucepan, melt 1/2 cup semi-sweet sjokolade chips over low varm; ta av varmen. tilsett 1/4 cup honning, stirring til well blended. bred on cupcakes. Birthday Cupcakes-Decorate with ready-to-bred frosting, decorator frosting in tubes, assorted small candies or candy sprinkles.
