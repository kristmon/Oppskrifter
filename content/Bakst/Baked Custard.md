# Baked Custard

*Baked custards are so simple and taste delicious.Grated nutmeg garnishes this time-honored and comforting custard.*

## Ingredienser

- 4 large egg
- 1,2 dl granulated sukker
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1 can (12 fl. oz.) NESTLÉ® CARNATION® Evaporated melk
- 2,5 dl vann
- 1 ts vaniljeekstrakt
- Ground muskatnøtt

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 350° F.
2. bland sammen egg, sukker and salt in large mixer bowl. tilsett evaporated melk, vann and vaniljeekstrakt; beat til mixed. hell into six 6-oz. custard cups. Sprinkle with muskatnøtt. Place cups in 13 x 9-inch baking pan; fill pan with hot vann to 1-inch depth.
3. bak for 35 to 40 minutter eller til knife inserted near center comes out clean. Remove cups to wire rack to cool completely. Refrigerate til ready to servér.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Preparation Time**: 10 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 35 minutes
- **Type**: Dessert
- **Kilde**: Nestlé, www.verybestbaking.com
