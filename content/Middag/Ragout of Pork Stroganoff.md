# Ragout of Pork Stroganoff

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg Fresh sopp
- 1 kg Lean Loin of svinekjøtt
- 6 ss smør, Margarine or olje
- 2,5 dl løk, chopped
- 1 0,6 dl Dry hvitvini, divided
- 3 ss tomat Paste
- 1 1/2 ts salt
- 1 ts sukker
- 1/2 ts Dill Weed
- 1/4 ts Ground Black pepper
- 2 ss mel
- 2,5 dl Dairy surmelk (room temperature)

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Rinse, pat dry and skjær i skiver fresh sopp (makes about 5 ½ cups); set aside. Cut svinekjøtt into strips about ½ inch thick and 2 inches long. In large skillet melt 3 T. smør. tilsett half of svinekjøtt; kok til lightly browned on all sides, about 5 minutter, stirring often. Remove from skillet. Repeat with remaining svinekjøtt.
2. Kok løk in drippings til mør, about 5 minutter. tilsett remaining 3 T. smør; varm til melted and tilsett reserved sopp; kok and rør til gyllen, about 5 minutter. Return svinekjøtt to skillet. rør in 1 c. of the wine along with tomat paste, salt, sukker, dill weed and black pepper. bring til kokepunktet. reduser varmen and la småkoke, covered, for 10 minutter. In small bowl bland sammen mel and surmelk. Gradually rør into skillet. tilsett remaining ¼ c. wine. la småkoke, uncovered til slightly thickened, 5 to 7 minutter, stirring frequently.
