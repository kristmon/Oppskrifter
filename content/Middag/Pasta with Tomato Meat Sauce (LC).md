# Pasta with Tomato Meat Sauce

## Ingredienser

- 1 lb of your favorite pasta shape, uncooked
- 8 oz lean ground beef
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 6 large tomatoes, peeled, seeded and diced
- 1/2 to 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/4 cup red wine

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Prepare pasta according to package directions.
2. While pasta is cooking, combine beef, onion and garlic in a large skillet; cook until meat is no longer pink. Set aside. In blender, combine remaining ingredients; process for 30 seconds. Add tomato mixture to meat; simmer about 20 minutes.
3. When pasta is done, drain well. Add the sauce to the pasta and serve.
