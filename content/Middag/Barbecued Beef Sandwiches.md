# Barbecued Beef Sandwiches

## Ingredienser

- 1 1/1 kg hakket storfekjøtt
- 1,9 dl selleri, chopped
- 1,9 dl løk, chopped
- 1,2 dl green paprika, chopped
- 1 can tomatsaus
- 0,6 dl ketchup
- 2 ss brunt sukker
- 2 ss prepared grillsaus
- 2 ss eddiksyre
- 1 ss prepared sennep
- 1 ss Crosse & Blackwell® worcestershiresaus
- 1 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts pepper
- 8 hamburger buns

## Fremgangsmåte

1. hakket storfekjøtt in a large skillet, stirring to crumble. tøm.
2. Tilsett chopped selleri, chopped løk, and chopped green paprika. kok for 5 minutter, eller til the løk is tender.
3. Rør in tomatsaus, ketchup, brunt sukker, prepared grillsaus, eddiksyre, prepared sennep, worcestershiresaus, salt, and pepper.
4. Cover and la småkoke on low for 1 time. servér on hamburger buns.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 8
- **Preparation Time**: 7 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 1 hour and 15 minutes
- **Total Time**: 1 hour and 22 minutes
- **Type**: Barbecue, Main Dish, Meat, Sandwich
