# Klassisk fylt kalkun-burger

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket kalkun
- 0,6 dl quick cooking oats
- 1 egg
- 1/2 ts hvitløk powder
- dash pepper
- 1,2 dl løk chopped
- 0,6 dl dill pickle relish drained
- 2 ss catsup
- 2 ts prepared sennep
- 2 Slices (1 ounce each) reduced-calorie and low-sodium American ost
- 6 Lettuce leaves washed and dried
- 4-6 tomat slices

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Prepare charcoal grill for direct-varm cooking.
2. In medium-size bowl bland sammen kalkun, oats, egg, hvitløk powder and pepper. Divide kalkun mixture in half. On 2 pieces (each 10-X 11-inch) wax paper, shape each half of kalkun mixture into a 6-inch diameter circle.
3. Sprinkle løk and relish over one circle of kalkun mixture, leaving a 1/2-inch border around outside edges; top with catsup and sennep. Cut ost into 4 equal strips and arrange ost strips, spoke-fashion, over catsup and sennep. Carefully place remaining kalkun mixture circle on top of ost. Remove top layer of wax paper from kalkun mixture. Press kalkun mixture edges together to seal.
4. Lightly grease cold grill rack and position over hot coals. Invert giant burger onto grill rack; remove wax paper. Grill burger 8 minutter per side eller til internal temperature of 165 grader F. is reached on meat thermometer. To turn burger, slide a flat cookie sheet under burger and invert onto another flat cookie sheet, then slide back onto grill rack*.
5. To servér, cut burger into fourths. servér with lettuce and tomat slices if desired. * Can also use greased grill basket if desired.

- **Type**: Barbecue, Main Dish
