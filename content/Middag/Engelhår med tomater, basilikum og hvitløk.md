# Engelhår med tomater, basilikum og hvitløk

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg Angel Hair or Capellini, uncooked
- 2 ss vegetarolje
- 1 ss minced hvitløk
- 5 cups tomater, diced
- 1/2 ts basilikum
- 1,9 dl low-sodium kyllingbuljong
- 5 ss Parmesan ost
- salt to taste
- Freshly ground pepper to taste

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Prepare pasta according to package directions; tøm.
2. Varm olje in a large skillet over medium-high varm. tilsett hvitløk and kok for one minutt. tilsett tomater, basilikum, salt and pepper. kok for 3 minutter.
3. Tilsett hot pasta to skillet; toss well. tilsett kyllingbuljong and rør.
4. Toss with Parmesan ost and servér immediately.
