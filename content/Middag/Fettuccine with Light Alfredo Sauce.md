# Fettuccine with Light Alfredo Sauce

## Ingredienser

- 1 lb Fettucine, Linguine or Spaghetti, uncooked
- 1 cup evaporated skim milk
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley
- 1/4 tsp white pepper
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 4 oz green onions, sliced (white parts only)
- White pepper to taste

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Prepare pasta according to package directions; drain. In a large saucepan, bring the evaporated milk to a simmer over moderate heat. Stir in the Parmesan cheese, green onions and parsley. As soon as it has melted, and the sauce is thick and creamy, pour over cooked pasta. Season to taste with white pepper.
