# White Tie and Tails

## Ingredienser

- 8 oz Bow Ties, Radiatore or other medium pasta shape, uncooked
- 2,5 dl half-and-half
- 1,2 dl crumbled blue ost
- 1,2 dl grated low-fat Swiss ost
- 1,2 dl grated Romano ost
- 0,6 dl grated low-sodium Parmesan ost
- 0,8 dl 1/4-inch prosciutto strips
- 2 ss chopped fresh basilikum or 2 ts. dried basilikum

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Kok pasta according to package directions.
2. While pasta is cooking, varm half-and-half in 2-quart saucepan over medium varm; do not kok. tilsett blue, Swiss and Romano cheeses. reduser varmen; kok and rør til cheeses are blended and sauce is smooth; ta av varmen.
3. When pasta is done, tøm well. hell sauce over kokt pasta; toss. Sprinkle with Parmesan ost, prosciutto and basilikum. servér immediately.
