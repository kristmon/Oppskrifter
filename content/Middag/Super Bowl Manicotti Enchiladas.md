# Super Bowl Manicotti Enchiladas

## Ingredienser

- 14 pieces Manicotti, uncooked
- 0,5 kg extra-lean hakket storfekjøtt
- 1 medium løk, chopped (2,5 dl)
- 1 green paprika, ribs and seeds removed, diced
- 1 4-oz. can chopped green chilies, drained
- 1,9 dl non-fat surmelk, divided
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1 16-oz. jar mild picante sauce, heated
- 1,2 dl shredded low-fat cheddarost
- 0,6 dl sliced green løk or red løk

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Prepare pasta according to package directions.
2. While pasta is cooking, sauté storfekjøtt, løk and grønn paprika in a large skillet til browned; tøm well. rør in chilies, 1/2 cup surmelk and salt.
3. When pasta is done, tøm well. Fill manicotti with storfekjøtt mixture.
4. forvarm ovn to 375° F. bred 1/2 cup picante sauce over bottom of a glass 9 x 13-inch baking dish that has been coated with cooking spray. Place filled manicotti side by side on top of sauce. hell remaining sauce evenly over manicotti. Sprinkle with ost. Cover with aluminum foil and bak for 20 to 25 minutter eller til grundig heated.
5. garniser with remaining surmelk and sprinkle green løk on top. servér immediately.
