# Spaghetti Sauce

## Ingredienser

- 2 ss vegetarolje divided
- 1/1 kg hakket kalkun
- 1 Small løk chopped
- 1 Small green paprika seeded and chopped
- 1 Clove hvitløk crushed
- 2 14-Ounce Cans plum tomater with juice
- 1 ts dried oregano
- 1/4 ts salt
- 1/4 ts pepper

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Varm 1 tablespoon olje in medium skillet over medium varm. tilsett kalkun and kok, breaking up with wooden spoon til no longer pink, about 5 minutter. Remove with a slotted spoon; reserve.
2. Tilsett remaining tablespoon of olje and saute løk, grønn paprika and hvitløk about 5 minutter.
3. Tilsett reserved kalkun, tomater with juice, oregano, salt and pepper. Break up tomater with wooden spoon and forsiktig la småkoke about 20 minutter.
4. servér hot over pasta.
