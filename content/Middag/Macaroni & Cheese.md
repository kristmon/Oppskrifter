# Macaroni & Cheese

*Macaroni and cheese like it's supposed to be, tasty with homemade goodness. Children and adults alike will love this classic!*

## Ingredienser

- 1 2/7,5 dl (about 7 oz.) dry small elbow macaroni, cooked and drained
- 2 ss cornstarch
- 1 ts salt
- 1/2 ts dry sennep
- 1/4 ts ground black pepper
- 1 can (12 fl. oz.) NESTLÉ® CARNATION® Evaporated melk
- 2,5 dl vann
- 2 ss smør or margarine
- 5 dl (8 oz.) shredded sharp cheddarost, divided

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 375° F. Grease 2-quart casserole dish.
2. bland sammen cornstarch, salt, sennep and pepper in medium saucepan. rør in evaporated melk, vann and smør. kok over medium-high varm, stirring konstant, til mixture comes to a kok. kok for 1 minutt. ta av varmen. rør in 1 1/2 cups ost til melted. tilsett macaroni; bland well. hell into prepared casserole dish. Top with remaining ost.
3. bak for 20 to 25 minutter eller til ost is melted and light brown.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Preparation Time**: 15 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
- **Type**: Main Dish, Pasta
