# Spicy Beef-Topped Potatoes

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket storfekjøtt
- 2 large baking poteter
- 1,2 dl chopped løk
- 12 ounces picante sauce
- 1/2 ts salt
- 2 (1 oz.) slices cheddarost, cut diagonally into quarters
- 1 green løk, sliced

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Scrub poteter; prick each with fork in several places. Microwave at high 8 to 10 minutter eller til mør, rotating 1/4 turn after 4 minutter. Let stand while preparing topping.
2. bland sammen hakket storfekjøtt and chopped løk; arrange in a ring in microwave-safe sieve or small colander. Place sieve in microwave-safe bowl; microwave at high 3 minutter. rør to break up storfekjøtt. Continue cooking at high 3 minutter; rør. hell off drippings. Place storfekjøtt in same bowl.
3. Tilsett picante sauce, stirring to bland sammen. Microwave at high 1 to 2 minutter eller til heated through.
4. Cut poteter in half lengthwise; break up and fluff pulp with fork. Sprinkle with salt.
5. Spoon an equal amount of storfekjøtt mixture over each potato half. Top each with an equal amount of ost; tent loosely with foil and let stand 1 minutt. garniser with sliced green løk.
