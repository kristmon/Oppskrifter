# Apres Ski Turkey Stew

## Ingredienser

- 1-1/1 kg kalkun thighs skinned, boned and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 ss vegetarolje
- 2,5 dl tomat peeled and cut into chunks
- 2,5 dl grønn paprika seeded and thinly sliced
- 1 clove hvitløk minced
- 2 ss fresh lemon juice
- 1 ts Italian seasoning
- salt to taste
- 5 dl ris, cooked
- pepper to taste

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 3-quart saucepan, over medium-high varm, saute kalkun thighs in olje til all sides are browned.
2. Tilsett tomat, grønn paprika, hvitløk, lemon juice, seasonings, salt and pepper. Cover, reduser varmen to low and la småkoke about 12 minutter eller til kalkun is done.
3. Kok til thickened. servér over ris, if desired.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Type**: Main Dish, Soup
- **Kilde**: The National Turkey Federation, www.turkeyfed.org
