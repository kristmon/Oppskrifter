# Turkey Mexican Stew

## Ingredienser

- 1-1/5 dl løk coarsely chopped
- 2,5 dl grønn paprika cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 Large clove hvitløk minced
- 1 ss margarine
- 1 Can (15 ounces) tomatsaus
- 2 ts chilipodd
- 1/2 ts kanel
- 1/2 ts salt
- 2-1/1 kg kalkun thighs skinned and boned
- 5 dl sweet poteter peeled and cut into 1/2-inch cubes

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 5-quart non-stick saucepan over medium-high varm, saute løk, grønn paprika and hvitløk in margarine til translucent and pepper is tender.
2. bland in tomatsaus, chilipodd, kanel and salt. Increase varm and bring mixture to kok. tilsett thighs, reduser varmen, cover and la småkoke 45 to 60 minutter.
3. Tilsett poteter; kok 30 minutter eller til a meat thermometer inserted in thickest part of meat registers 180-185 grader F.
4. If desired, servér over ris or with cornbread.
