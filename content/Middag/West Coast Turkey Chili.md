# West Coast Turkey Chili

## Ingredienser

- 2,5 dl grønn paprika seeded and cut into 1/4 inch cubes
- 1-1/10 dl løk coarsely chopped
- 2 Cloves hvitløk minced
- 3 ss canola olje
- 2 Cans (15-1/2 ounces) røde bønner rinsed and well drained
- 1 Can (28 ounces) stewed tomater crushed
- 2,5 dl dry rødvin
- 7,5 dl cooked kalkun cut into 1/2 inch cubes
- 1 ss chilipodd
- 1 ss fresh koriander coarsely chopped
- 1 ts crushed rød paprika
- 1/2 ts salt

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a 3-quart saucepan over medium-high varm, saute grønn paprika, løk and hvitløk in olje 5 minutter eller til vegetables are tender-crisp.
2. Tilsett bønner, tomater, wine, kalkun, chilipodd, koriander, rød paprika and salt. Increase varm and bring mixture to kok; reduser varmen and la småkoke, uncovered, 25 minutter.
3. To servér, ladle into bowls and garniser with additional løk or fresh koriander.
