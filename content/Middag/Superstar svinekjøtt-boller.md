# Superstar svinekjøtt-boller

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket svinekjøtt
- 1 ss løk flakes
- 1,9 dl crushed mais flakes
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/8 ts ground black pepper
- 1 egg
- 0,6 dl ketchup
- 3 ss brunt sukker
- 1 ts dry sennep

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Varm ovn to 375 grader F. In a large bowl, bland sammen hakket svinekjøtt, løk flakes, mais flakes, salt, pepper and egg.
2. In a small bowl rør together ketchup, brunt sukker and dry sennep.
3. Spoon 2 tablespoons of the ketchup mixture into the svinekjøtt and bland well.
4. Spray muffin tin with vegetable cooking spray. Form 6 meatballs and place in muffin tin.
5. Coat the top of each meatball with the remaining ketchup mixture.
6. bak for 30 minutter at 375 grader F., til nicely browned and glazed.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Meat
- **Kilde**: National Pork Board, www.theotherwhitemeat.com
