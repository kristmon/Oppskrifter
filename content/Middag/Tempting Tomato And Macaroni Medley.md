# Tempting Tomato And Macaroni Medley

## Ingredienser

- 1 3/4 cups uncooked elbow macaroni
- 4 cups diced peeled tomatoes
- 1 Tbs corn oil, plus
- 1 tsp corn oil
- 1/4 tsp freshly-ground black pepper
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp garlic powder

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a large pan, cook macaroni according to package directions, omitting salt if called for. Drain macaroni, return to pan, and add remaining ingredients.
2. Simmer uncovered over low-medium heat for 20 minutes or until tomatoes have cooked down stir gently to prevent sticking.
