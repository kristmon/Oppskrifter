# Batter Fried Chicken

*Our Batter-Fried Chicken will have all of your chickadees cluck-cluck-clucking with happy contentment. Cooked in a minimum of oil this chicken is tender and juicy but never greasy!*

## Ingredienser

- 1 1/10 dl mel
- 1 ts salt
- 1 ts bakepulver
- 1/2 ts dried timian
- 1/4 ts ground black pepper
- 1 egg
- 1,9 dl 2% melk
- 1,2 dl vegetarolje, divided
- 1 fresh whole kylling, cut up

## Fremgangsmåte

1. bland sammen mel, salt, bakepulver, timian and pepper in a medium-sized mixing bowl; set aside.
2. Beat egg in a small bowl til frothy. tilsett melk and 2 tablespoons olje; beat til well blended. hell into mel mixture; rør til well blended.
3. Varm remaining 6 tablespoons olje over medium varm in a large skillet. Dip kylling pieces in batter; shake off any excess. stekk kylling for about 10 minutter, turning til lightly browned all over.
4. Lower varm to very low, cover tightly and la småkoke for about 30 minutter. Turn pieces periodically to ensure even cooking; tilsett 1-2 tablespoons vann during cooking if pan becomes too dry. Uncover during final 4-5 minutter to allow skin to become crisp. servér hot or cold.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Preparation Time**: 15 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 40 minutes
- **Type**: Main Dish, Poultry
