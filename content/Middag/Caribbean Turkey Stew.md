# Caribbean Turkey Stew

## Ingredienser

- 1 kg kalkun thighs skin removed
- 1 ss olje
- 7,5 dl løk thinly sliced
- 1/2 ts rød paprika flakes
- 1/2 ts salt
- 0,6 dl sweetened flaked coconut
- 2,5 dl kalkun broth
- 1 Can (16 ounces) stewed tomater drained
- 1 Can (16 ounces) sorte bønner drained
- 0,5 kg butternut squash peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 0,5 kg sweet poteter peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 2 Medium bananas sliced
- 10 Green løk sliced
- 1,2 dl sweetened flaked coconut
- 1 to 2 Limes cut into wedges

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 5-quart saucepan, over medium-high varm, brown thighs in olje about 3 minutter per side. Remove and set aside.
2. In same saucepan, saute løk 2 to 3 minutter eller til translucent. tilsett rød paprika, salt, coconut, broth, tomater, sorte bønner, squash, sweet poteter and kalkun thighs. Bring mixture to kok; reduser varmen, cover and la småkoke 1-1/4 to 1-1/2 timer, eller til kalkun thighs register 180-185 grader F. on meat thermometer inserted into thickest portion.
3. Ten minutter before serving, remove kalkun thighs from stew and strip meat from bones with fork; return meat strips to stew. varm throughout.
4. To servér, spoon stew into bowls and garniser with bananas, green løk and coconut. Squeeze juice of lime over top.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Main Dish, Soup
