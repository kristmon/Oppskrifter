# Veal Parmigiana

## Ingredienser

- 1,9 dl Italian-style bread crumbs
- 1,9 dl Parmesan ost
- salt
- pepper
- 1 egg beaten
- 1/8 cup vann
- 6 (4 oz.) Veal Slices, thawed and pounded
- 4 ss Clarified smør
- 3 slices mozzarella ost
**Sauce**
- 2 ss olivenolje
- 1 small løk, chopped
- 2 medium cloves hvitløk, minced
- 1 (6 oz.) can tomat paste
- 0,8 dl vann
- 1 (28 oz.) can crushed tomater
- 1 ts basilikum
- 1 ss sukker

## Fremgangsmåte

1. bland sammen bread crumbs, Parmesan ost and seasoning. Beat egg and bland sammen with vann. Dip veal into beaten egg, then coat with dry mixture. Quickly sauté and place on ovn broiler.
2. Top each piece with a skjær i skiver of mozzarella ost. Broil til ost begins to bubble. hell 1/3 cup of sauce on each serving.
3. Sauté løk and hvitløk in olje til løk is translucent. rør in tomat paste and vann. tilsett tomater, basilikum, and sukker. la småkoke covered for 1/2 time.
