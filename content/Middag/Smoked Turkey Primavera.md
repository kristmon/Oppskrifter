# Smoked Turkey Primavera

## Ingredienser

- 6 cups fresh winter vegetables i.e. broccoli, cauliflower, gulrøtter, sopp
- 2 ss margarine
- 1,2 dl reduced-sodium kylling bouillon
- 1,2 dl skim melk
- 1,2 dl plus 2 ss Parmesan ost grated
- 10 oz spaghetti or linguini cooked
- 0,5 kg smoked kalkun cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Cut broccoli and cauliflower into florets. skjær i skiver gulrøtter and stemmed sopp into medium slices.
2. In large skillet, over medium-high varm, saute vegetables in margarine til crisp-tender. tilsett bouillon and melk. kok 1 to 2 minutter eller til bubbly. Fold in 1/2 cup ost, kokt pasta and kalkun. kok 1 to 2 minutter eller til mixture is heated throughout.
3. servér on large platter, topped with remaining ost and pepper to taste.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Main Dish, Pasta
- **Kilde**: The National Turkey Federation, www.turkeyfed.org
