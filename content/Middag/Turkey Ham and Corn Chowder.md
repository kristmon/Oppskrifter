# Turkey Ham and Corn Chowder

## Ingredienser

- 2,5 dl løk chopped
- 1 ss vegetarolje
- 3 ss mel
- 1/2 ts black pepper
- 1 quart skim melk
- 5 dl frozen hash brown poteter
- 2 Cans (11 ounces each) mais with red and green peppers drained
- 3/4 kg kalkun skinke cut into 1/2-inch cubes

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 3-quart saucepan, over medium-high varm, saute løk in hot olje for 3 minutter eller til translucent. bland in mel and pepper. kok for a few minutter til lightly golden brown. Remove pan from varm; slowly tilsett melk, stirring konstant.
2. Return pan to varm and rør til thickened. tilsett poteter; bring mixture to kok, stirring konstant. tilsett mais and kalkun skinke; return mixture to kok.
3. Immediately reduser varmen to low and la småkoke soup 15 minutter eller til poteter are tender and mixture has thickened.
