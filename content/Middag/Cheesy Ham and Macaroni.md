# Cheesy Ham and Macaroni

## Ingredienser

- 1 1.8 oz package white sauce bland
- 5 dl melk
- 1,2 dl grated Parmesan ost
- 1,2 dl cubed American ost
- 1/8 ts ground pepper
- 7 oz macaroni, cooked according to directions, drained
- 1 1,2 dl fully-cooked skinke
- 2,5 dl frozen green peas

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Thaw the frozen peas. In a large saucepan rør together white sauce bland and melk.* Following package directions, kok til thickened.
2. Rør in ost and pepper.
3. Tilsett macaroni, skinke and peas and kok, stirring til heated through.
4. servér hot.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Main Dish, Meat, Pasta
