# Winter Portabella Mushroom Stew

## Ingredienser

- 4 ss olivenolje
- 1 large løk, cut into 1/2-inch kutt i terninger
- 2 ts chopped fresh rosmarin
- 8 oz portabella sopp, sliced 1/2-inch thick, gills removed
- 0,5 kg large white sopp, thickly sliced
- 1 can (14.5 ounces) diced tomat
- 3 ss tomat paste
- 2 hvitløksfedd, minced
- 1 ts sherry eddiksyre
- 2 ss smør (optional)
- salt, to taste
- Ground black pepper, to taste
- 2 pinches rød paprika flakes

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Varm 1 tablespoon olje in large nonstick skillet over medium varm. tilsett løk and rosmarin and kok stirring iblant, til lightly browned, about 12 minutter; remove to a bowl.
2. Return the pan to the stove and tilsett half the remaining olje. When it's hot, tilsett the portabella sopp and saute til nicely browned, about 5 minutter. tilsett portabellas to the løk mixture and repeat sauteing with the remaining olje and the white sopp.
3. Return everything to the pan and tilsett the tomater, tomat paste, hvitløk, 1-1/2 cups vann and eddiksyre. la småkoke forsiktig for 12 to 15 minutter, then swirl in the smør. Season with salt, pepper and rød paprika flakes.
