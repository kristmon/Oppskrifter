# Vegetarian Spaghetti Pizza

## Ingredienser

- 8 oz Spaghetti, uncooked
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup skim milk
- 3 cups shredded part-skim mozzarella cheese, divided
- 8 oz fresh spinach, cooked and chopped, or 1 10-oz. package frozen spinach, defrosted and drained well
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 32-oz. jar spaghetti sauce, uncooked
**Topping**
- 2 cups cooked broccoli florets
- 1 medium zucchini, sliced into thin

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Break spaghetti into 2-inch pieces and cook according to package directions; drain. In a large bowl, beat eggs. Gradually add milk, 1 cup mozzarella cheese, spinach and garlic powder. Stir in spaghetti pieces. Lightly oil a 10 1/2 x 15 1/2 x 1 1/2-inch baking pan. Spread spaghetti mixture into pan, building up sides slightly. Bake at 425° F for 15 minutes. Remove from oven.
2. Spread spaghetti sauce over spaghetti "crust." Spread broccoli and zucchini over sauce and sprinkle with remaining mozzarella cheese. Bake at 350° F for 35 minutes. Let stand 5 minutes before cutting.
