# Saucy Texas Ribs

## Ingredienser

- 3-4 Texas Red Grapefruit, halved
- 1,2 dl tomat paste
- 1,2 dl catsup
- 1 ss cornstarch
- 1/4 ts liquid hot pepper sauce
- 12 storfekjøtt rib bones (about 3 pounds)
- 2 egg, lightly beaten
- salt
- pepper
- 2,5 dl fine fresh bread crumbs
- pepper
- 0,6 dl melted smør or margarine

## Fremgangsmåte

1. First, prepare catsup; juice enough grapefruit to make 2 cups. hell juice into saucepan along with tomat paste, catsup, cornstarch and hot pepper sauce. bring til kokepunktet, stirring konstant; kok 2 to 3 minutter. Keep warm.
2. Dip ribs in beaten egg; then roll in seasoned crumbs. Arrange on foil covered broiler pan. Drizzle smør over ribs.
3. Broil 4 to 6 inches from varm til crisp and brown (about 10 minutter), turning frequently and basting with drippings.
4. servér with warm grapefruit catsup as a dip.
