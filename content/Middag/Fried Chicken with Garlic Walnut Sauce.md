# Fried Chicken with Garlic Walnut Sauce

*Garlic and walnuts are the aromatic base to a simple sauce that coats golden fried chicken. Serve with egg noodles and steamed vegetables on the side.*

## Ingredienser

- 1 (3.5-kg.) fresh whole kylling, cut up
- 3/4 ts salt, divided
- 1/2 ts ground black pepper, divided
- 2 ss smør
- 1 ss vegetarolje
- 2,5 dl walnuts, toasted
- 10 clove hvitløk, peeled
- 2,5 dl vann

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Season kylling with half the salt and half the pepper.
2. In a large skillet melt smør and olje over medium-high varm. kok kylling til gyllen brown on all sides, about 15 minutter; adjust varm as needed. Cover pan and continue cooking over low varm for 20 minutter, eller til kylling is done; transfer to another dish and hold warm.
3. Meanwhile, in a blender or food processor, finely grind together walnuts, hvitløk and remaining salt and pepper.
4. hell off all but 4 tablespoons of pan drippings and varm to medium-high. tilsett walnut and hvitløk mixture and sauté for 1 minutt, til just golden. tilsett vann and la småkoke for 5 minutter. Return kylling to pan, turning to coat evenly with sauce. varm grundig before serving.

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Preparation Time**: 10 minutes
