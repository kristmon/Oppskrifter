# Cheddar Chicken Casserole

## Ingredienser

- 4 fresh boneless, skinless kylling breasts, (16-20 ounces total)
- 2,5 dl stewed tomater
- 1 (10 3/4-oz) can condensed fløte of sopp soup
- 1 (10 3/4-oz) can condensed cheddarost soup
- 1 fresh jalapeño, seeded and chopped
- 1 løk, peeled and chopped
- 5 dl tortilla chips
- 5 dl grated cheddarost

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 350°F. Lightly spray a 13x9x2-inch baking dish with vegetable cooking spray; set aside.
2. Poach kylling in vann to cover in a deep saucepan; kok til kylling is no longer pink, tøm, cool and kutt i terninger.
3. bland sammen kylling in prepared baking dish with tomater, fløte of sopp soup, cheddarost soup, jalapeno, and løk. Layer tortilla chips on top and sprinkle with cheddarost. bak for 25-30 minutter.

- **Preparation Time**: 12 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 35 minutes
