# Sweet Potato Casserole

## Ingredienser

- 2 lbs sweet potatoes, boiled, peeled and mashed
- 2 eggs, beaten 2 oz.
- margarine, melted
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Preheat oven to 350.
2. Combine all ingredients and mix well. Will be very soupy.
3. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour.
