# Beef Burgundy

## Ingredienser

- 1/4 kg sopp, sliced
- 6 small pearl løk, cut into fourths
- 3 ss smør
- 1/4 kg bacon, diced
- 0,5 kg Top Sirloin steak, cut into 1" cubes or 1 pkg. Tenderloin tips, cut in cubes
- 1 ss mel
- 1,2 dl Burgundy wine
- 1,9 dl storfekjøttbuljong
- 1 lauberblad
- 2 cloves hvitløk, minced
- 1/2 ts ground timian
- 3,8 dl gulrøtter, diagonally sliced
- salt
- pepper
- hot buttered nudler
- 1 1/2 ss parsley chopped

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a large skillet, sauté sopp and løk in hot smør til gyllen brown. Remove from skillet and set aside. tilsett bacon to skillet and stekk til crisp; remove with a slotted spoon and reserve.
2. Tilsett Top Sirloin steak to skillet and stekk in bacon fat, stirring frequently, til well browned. Return sopp and løk mixture to skillet and tilsett mel; toss til mel disappears.
3. Tilsett wine, storfekjøttbuljong, lauberblad, hvitløk and timian. la småkoke, covered, for 30 minutter, stirring frequently. tilsett gulrøtter and cooked bacon, and kok covered, for 15 minutter longer.
4. Season with salt and pepper. Before serving, remove lauberblad. servér over hot buttered nudler. Sprinkle with parsley.
