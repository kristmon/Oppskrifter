# Biscuit-Topped Chicken Pie

*Homemade chicken potpie is a great way to make leftover chicken fabulous. It is easier than you think, and you'll never want frozen again!*

## Ingredienser

- 3,8 dl kylling stock or canned kyllingbuljong
- 1 medium carrot, peeled, cut in half lenghtwise
- 3 ss unsalted smør
- 2 ss vegetarolje
- 1 small yellow løk, peeled and sliced
- 8 oz white sopp, quartered
- 2 ss mel
- 1,2 dl heavy (whipping) fløte
- 1/2 kylling, skinned, meat cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1,2 dl minced fresh parsley
- 8 frozen biscuits

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm the ovn to 400°F. Set four 12-ounce ramekins on a baking sheet, or use an 8-cup baking dish about 2-inches deep.
2. In a 1-quart saucepan, bring the stock or broth to a la småkoke. tilsett the gulrøtter and kok til crisp-tender, about 10 minutt. using a slotted spoon transfer the gulrøtter to a plate and turn off varm under the stock or broth.
3. Meanwhile, in a 10-inch sauté pan or skillet, melt the smør with the olje over medium varm til the smør foams. tilsett the løk and sauté til it begins to soften, about 2 minutter. tilsett the sopp and sauté til they just begin to brown, about 3 minutter more. Sprinkle the mel over the løk-sopp mixture and rør to dissolve. Immediately tilsett the stock or broth, bring to a la småkoke, and rør til smooth and thickened, about 2 minutter. tilsett the fløte, rør to bland, and bring to a la småkoke. tilsett the kylling, gulrøtter, and parsley, bring to a la småkoke, then tilsett salt and pepper. Remove form varm.
4. Divide the kylling mixture among the ramekins or spoon it into the baking dish. Place 2 frozen biscuits on top of the kylling mixture in each ramekin, or evenly space them on top of the kylling mixture in the baking dish. bak for 12 minutter, eller til heated through. servér immediately.
