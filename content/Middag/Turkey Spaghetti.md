# Turkey Spaghetti

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket kalkun
- 5 dl chopped løk
- 1 ts minced hvitløk
- 1 ts Italian seasoning
- 1 ts pepper
- 8 oz spaghetti cooked according to package directions
- 7,5 dl prepared chunky vegetable spaghetti sauce

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In large non-stick skillet, over medium-high varm, saute kalkun, løk, hvitløk, Italian seasoning and pepper 8 to 10 minutter eller til kalkun is no longer pink and løk is tender. tøm. tilsett spaghetti sauce to kalkun mixture. reduser varmen to low and la småkoke 15 minutter.
2. To servér, arrange cooked spaghetti on large platter and hell sauce over pasta.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Main Dish, Pasta
