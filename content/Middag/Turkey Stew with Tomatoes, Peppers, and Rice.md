# Turkey Stew with Tomatoes, Peppers, and Rice

## Ingredienser

- 1 ss olivenolje
- 1-1/1 kg kalkun thighs boneless, skinless, quartered
- 1/4 ts salt
- 1/4 ts freshly ground pepper
- 2,5 dl løk chopped
- 1,2 dl red paprika seeded and sliced
- 1,2 dl green paprika seeded and sliced
- 14 oz kalkun broth
- 1 Can (14-1/2 ounces) whole peeled tomater with juice
- 1-1/7,5 dl white ris uncooked
- 2,5 dl frozen green peas thawed

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Varm olje in large skillet. tilsett kalkun and kok til brown; season with salt and pepper.
2. Rør in løk; cover tightly and kok over low varm, 10 minutter.
3. Rør in peppers, broth, tomater with juice, and ris; varm to boiling, stirring to break up tomater.
4. Cover and kok over low varm 20 minutter eller til liquid is absorbed. Sprinkle peas on top; cover and kok 5 minutter more.
