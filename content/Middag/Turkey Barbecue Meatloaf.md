# Turkey Barbecue Meatloaf

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket kalkun
- 2,5 dl chopped løk
- 1,2 dl seasoned bread crumbs
- 1,2 dl grated gulrøtter
- 1,2 dl bottled grillsaus divided
- 2 ts worcestershiresaus
- 1 ts minced hvitløk
- 3/4 ts pepper
- Vegetable cooking spray

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In medium bowl bland sammen kalkun, løk, bread crumbs, gulrøtter, 1/4 cup grillsaus, Worcestershire, hvitløk and pepper.
2. In 9-inch pie plate, sprayed with vegetable cooking spray, shape meat mixture into round loaf. Drizzle top of loaf with remaining grillsaus. bak at 350 grader F. 35 to 45 minutter eller til meat thermometer reaches 160 grader F. when inserted in center of meatloaf, juices run clear and meat is no longer pink.

- **Type**: Main Dish, Poultry
