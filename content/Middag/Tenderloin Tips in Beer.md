# Tenderloin Tips in Beer

## Ingredienser

- 2 (0,5 kg.) pkgs. Tenderloin Tips
- salt
- pepper
- 1,2 dl mel
- olje for sautéing
- 1 large løk, sliced
- 1 leek, white only, chopped
- 1 whole shallot, finely chopped
- 2 hvitløksfedd, minced
- 1 (12 oz.) can beer
- 1/4 ts timian
- 1 lauberblad

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Thaw Tenderloin Tips. Season with salt and pepper; shake in bag containing the mel. hell a thin film of olje in skillet and sauté the løk, leek, shallot and hvitløk til mør. Remove sautéed mixture with a slotted spoon and place in a casserole dish.
2. In the same pan, brown meat on all sides, adding more olje as needed. You may have to do this in 2 or 3 batches. tilsett browned meat to casserole.
3. hell half the can of beer into the pan in which the meat was browned. bland with the pan juices, scraping sides and bottom of pan. hell this over meat in casserole. tilsett remaining beer, timian and lauberblad. Cover tightly and la småkoke 45-60 minutter on top of the stove.
