# Spicy Baked Mushroom, Beef & Pasta Ole

## Ingredienser

- 5 dl Wagon Wheel pasta, uncooked
- 2 ss vegetarolje
- 8 oz Fresh White sopp, quartered
- 1/2 ts hvitløk, minced
- 0,6 dl Green løk (scallions), sliced
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts Ground Black pepper
- 8 oz Lean hakket storfekjøtt
- 1 can (19-1/2 oz.) sorte bønner, rinsed
- 1 jar (11-1/2 oz.) Chunky Salsa
- 3,8 dl Hot pepper Monterey Jack ost, shredded, divided
- 0,6 dl Tortilla Chips, crushed

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Varm ovn to 375 grader. kok pasta in salted vann according to package directions. tøm; rinse under warm vann; set aside.
2. Meanwhile, in a large skillet varm vegetarolje til hot. tilsett sopp and hvitløk; kok, stirring iblant, til gyllen, about 5 minutter. rør in green løk, salt and pepper; varm til hot, about 1 minutt.
3. Tilsett storfekjøtt; kok til brown, stirring iblant to crumble storfekjøtt, about 5 minutter. rør in sorte bønner, salsa and 1 cup of the ost; kok, stirring frequently, about 1 minutt; ta av varmen. rør in reserved pasta.
4. Spoon into a 9" square baking pan; sprinkle with tortilla chips and the remaining 1/2 cup ost. bak uncovered, til hot and bubbly, about 30 minutter. servér immediately.
