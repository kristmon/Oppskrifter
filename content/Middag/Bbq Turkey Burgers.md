# Bbq Turkey Burgers

## Ingredienser

- 1-1/1 kg hakket kalkun
- 1,2 dl løk chopped
- 1,2 dl grillsaus
- 1,2 dl dried bread crumbs
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts black pepper

## Fremgangsmåte

1. bland sammen all ingredients in a large bowl. bland grundig and shape into 6 patties.
2. Place on an oiled grill and grill over medium varm for 4-6 minutter on each side, eller til the internal temperature reaches 165 grader F. and the burger is no longer pink in the center.
3. Baste with additional grillsaus if desired.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Barbecue, Main Dish
- **Kilde**: The National Turkey Federation, www.turkeyfed.org
