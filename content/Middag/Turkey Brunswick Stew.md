# Turkey Brunswick Stew

## Ingredienser

- 1-1/1 kg kalkun thighs skin removed
- 5 to 6 cups vann to cover
- 2 bay leaves
- 5 peppercorns
- 3 sprigs parsley
- 1 stalk selleri cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 large red-skinned potato cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 large løk cut in half and quartered
- 1 large carrot peeled and cut into 1/2-inch slices
- 1 14-ounce can tomater drained
- 1 10-ounce package frozen kernel mais
- 1 10-ounce package frozen lima bønner
- 0,6 dl dried tart cherries
- 1 ss sukker
- 1-1/2 ts salt
- 1 ts hvitløk minced
- 1/2 ts coarsley ground pepper
- 1/2 ts dried oregano
- 1/8 ts ground mace
- 2,5 dl wild ris cooked according to package directions

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 5-quart saucepan over high varm, bring til kokepunktet thighs, vann, bay leaves, peppercorns, parsley and selleri. Skim foam from top of liquid. Cover, reduser varmen and la småkoke for 45 minutter.
2. Remove and discard bay leaves and parsley. Remove thighs and allow to cool enough to handle. Remove kalkun from the bone and cut into large chunks. Discard bones.
3. Return kalkun to pan along with potato, løk, carrot, tomater, mais, lima bønner, cherries, sukker, salt, hvitløk, pepper, oregano and mace. bring til kokepunktet; reduser varmen and la småkoke 30 minutter eller til vegetables are tender.
4. Rør in cooked wild ris.

## Info

- **Type**: Main Dish, Soup
