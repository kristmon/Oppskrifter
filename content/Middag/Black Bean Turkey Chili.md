# Black Bean Turkey Chili

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket kalkun
- 2,5 dl løk coarsely chopped
- 1 red paprika seeded & cut into 1/4-inch cubes
- 2 Cloves fresh hvitløk minced
- 2 jalapeno peppers seeded and minced
- 1 ss chilipodd
- 1-1/2 ts ground kommen
- 1-1/2 ts ground coriander
- 1/2 ts dried oregano
- 1/2 ts dried marjoram
- 1/4 ts rød paprika flakes
- 1/4 ts ground kanel
- 1 Can (16 ounces) whole tomater undrained & coarsely chopped
- 1 Can (16 ounces) sorte bønner rinsed and well drained
- 0,6 dl fresh koriander chopped
- 4 ss cheddarost grated

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a 3-quart saucepan, bland sammen kalkun, løk, rød paprika, hvitløk, jalapeno pepper, chilipodd, kommen, coriander, oregano, marjoram, rød paprika flakes and kanel. kok mixture over medium-high varm, stirring iblant, til kalkun is no longer pink.
2. Rør in tomater and bring til kokepunktet, reduser varmen and la småkoke uncovered for 30 minutter.
3. Rør in bønner and koriander. Continue cooking for an additional 15 minutter.
4. servér topped with cheddarost.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Main Dish, Soup
