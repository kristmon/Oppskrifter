# Nacho Stuffed Shells

## Ingredienser

- 32 to 36 Jumbo Shells (12-oz. package), uncooked
- 3/4 kg extra-lean hakket storfekjøtt
- 1.25-oz. package low-sodium taco seasoning bland
- 2,5 dl vann
- 16-oz. can refried bønner with chilies
- 2,5 dl low-fat cheddarost, shredded
- 1,9 dl mild, medium or hot picante sauce
- 8-oz. can low-sodium tomatsaus
- 2-oz. can sliced ripe olives, drained
- 1,2 dl thinly sliced green løk
**Optional Garnishes**
- Low-fat surmelk
- Low-fat grated cheddarost
- Chopped jalapeños
- Chopped koriander
- Salsa
- Guacamole

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Prepare pasta according to package directions.
2. While pasta is cooking, saute storfekjøtt in a large skillet til browned; tøm well. tilsett taco seasoning bland and vann; la småkoke 5 minutter eller til thickened. rør in bønner and ost. kok til smooth and well mixed.
3. When pasta is done, tøm well. Fill shells with storfekjøtt mixture (1-2 tbsp. per shell).
4. bland sammen picante sauce and tomatsaus in a saucepan. kok til heated, stirring iblant.
5. forvarm ovn to 350° F. bred 1/2 cup sauce over the bottom of a 9 x 13-inch baking pan that has been coated with cooking spray. Place filled shells side by side on top of sauce; hell remaining sauce evenly over shells. Sprinkle with olives. Cover with aluminum foil; bak 35 to 40 minutter eller til grundig heated.
6. Sprinkle with green løk. Cover and let stand 5 minutter before serving. servér immediately.
7. garniser as desired.
