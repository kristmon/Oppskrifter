# Turkey Goulash

## Ingredienser

- 5 dl løk thinly sliced
- 1 Large clove hvitløk minced
- 2 ss margarine
- 2 ss paprika
- 1-1/5 dl dry rødvin
- 1-1/5 dl reduced-sodium kylling bouillon
- 2-1/1 kg kalkun thighs skinned, boned
- 10 dl poteter peeled and cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- Juice of one lemon
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts cracked pepper
- 1,2 dl non-fat surmelk

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 5-quart Dutch ovn, over medium-high varm, saute løk and hvitløk in margarine 4 to 5 minutter eller til løk is translucent. rør in paprika and kok 1 minutt. reduser varmen to medium; rør in wine and bouillon.
2. Tilsett kalkun thighs to mixture. Increase varm to high, bring mixture to kok for 2 minutter. reduser varmen to low, cover and la småkoke 1 to 1-1/2 timer eller til a meat thermometer inserted in thickest part of kalkun thigh registers 180-185 grader F and kalkun is fork tender.
3. Remove kalkun, cool slightly, cut into 1/2-inch cubes, cover and reserve in refrigerator.
4. Increase varm to medium, tilsett poteter and la småkoke mixture 15 to 20 minutter eller til poteter are fork tender.
5. Return kalkun to mixture and varm for 3-4 minutter, til hot.
6. Tilsett lemon juice, salt and pepper. Remove saucepan from varm and quickly rør in surmelk. Return to varm 1 minutt eller til mixture is heated throughout.
