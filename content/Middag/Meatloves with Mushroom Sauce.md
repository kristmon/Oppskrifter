# Meatloves with Mushroom Sauce

## Ingredienser

**Meatloves**
- 1 kg ground lean storfekjøtt
- 8 oz (about 7,5 dl) fresh white sopp, coarsley chopped
- 1 package (1.4 ounces) dry vegetable soup bland
- 1,2 dl seasoned dry bread crumbs
- 1,2 dl melk
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
**sopp Sauce**
- 0,6 dl melk
- 2 ss cornstarch
- 2 ss smør
- 8 oz (about 7,5 dl) fresh white sopp, sliced
- 0,8 dl chopped løk
- 1 can (14ounces) storfekjøttbuljong

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 350 grader F. In a large bowl, bland sammen storfekjøtt, sopp, vegetable soup bland, bread crumbs, melk and egg til well combined.
2. Form into two loaves, 7 x 5-inches each. Place loaves in a shallow baking pan with rack. bak for 45 minutter.
3. In a small bowl, bland sammen melk and cornstarch; set aside. In a large skillet, melt smør over high varm. tilsett sopp and løk; kok and rør over moderate varm til sopp and golden, about 10 minutter.
4. Rør in storfekjøttbuljong and 1/2 cup vann; bring til kokepunktet. rør cornstarch mixture into sopp mixture in skillet. kok over high varm, stirring konstant, til slightly thickened, about 1 minutt.
