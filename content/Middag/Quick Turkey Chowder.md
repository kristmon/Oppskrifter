# Quick Turkey Chowder

## Ingredienser

- 2 ss smør
- 1 small løk diced
- 1 selleri stalk diced
- 2 gulrøtter peeled and diced
- 2 ss all-purpose mel
- 7,5 dl kalkun stock
- 5 dl cooked kalkun chopped
- 1 potato peeled and diced
- 1,9 dl frozen mais
- 1/2 ts dried timian
- salt to taste
- pepper to taste

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a saute pan, varm smør and kok løk, selleri and gulrøtter for one minutt. ta av varmen and whip in mel. Continue to kok and rør on low varm for one minutt. rør in kalkun stock and bring til kokepunktet.
2. reduser varmen and tilsett kalkun, potato, mais and timian. kok over low varm for 15 minutter eller til the potato is cooked. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
