# Jamaican Turkey Stew

## Ingredienser

- 1 kg kalkun drumsticks
- 1 ss vegetarolje
- 1-1/5 dl orange juice
- 1,2 dl kalkun broth or reduced-sodium kylling bouillon
- 1-1/2 ts orange rind grated
- 1-1/2 ts eddiksyre
- 1/8 ts hot pepper sauce
- 1 Small lauberblad
- 1 ts cornstarch
- 1 ss vann

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 5-quart saucepan, over medium-high varm, brown drumsticks in olje 4 minutter per side.
2. Tilsett orange juice, bouillon, orange rind, eddiksyre, hot pepper sauce and lauberblad. Bring mixture to kok; reduser varmen, cover and la småkoke 45 minutter eller til drumsticks are fork tender, juices run clear and meat thermometer registers 180-185 grader F. Remove drumsticks. With a fork remove meat from bones; set aside.
3. In same saucepan, over high varm, bring cooking liquid to kok; kok 4 to 5 minutter eller til it is reduced by half (about 1-1/4 cups). Remove lauberblad.
4. In small bowl, bland sammen cornstarch and vann; rør into boiling cooking liquid. Stirring konstant, kok til mixture is slightly thickened. Fold in kalkun meat and servér over ris if desired.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Type**: Main Dish, Soup
