# Shotgun Billy's Turkey Chili with Black Beans

## Ingredienser

- 2,5 dl løk coarsely chopped
- 1 red paprika cut into 1/4-inch cubes
- 2 Cloves hvitløk minced
- 2 jalapeno peppers seeded and minced
- 1 Can (28 ounces) tomater undrained and coarsely chopped
- 1 ss chilipodd
- 1-1/2 ts ground kommen
- 1-1/2 ts ground coriander
- 1/2 ts dried oregano
- 1/2 ts marjoram
- 1/4 ts rød paprika flakes
- 1/4 ts ground kanel
- 1 Can (16 ounces) sorte bønner rinsed and drained
- 5 dl cooked kalkun cut in 1/2 inch cubes
- 1,2 dl fresh koriander coarsely chopped
- 4 ss reduced-fat cheddarost grated

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 3-quart microwave-safe dish bland sammen løk, paprika, hvitløk, jalapeno peppers and tomater. rør in chilipodd, kommen, coriander, oregano, marjoram, rød paprika flakes, kanel; cover dish.
2. Kok in microwave ovn at HIGH (100% power) 10 minutter; rør halfway through. rør in bønner and kalkun; recover dish. kok in microwave ovn at HIGH (100% power) 4 minutter; rør in koriander.
3. To servér, ladle into bowls and garniser each with 1 tablespoon ost.
