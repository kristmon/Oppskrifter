# En strålende chili

## Ingredienser

- 1 package (15 oz.) firm tofu
- 1 ss vegetarolje
- 2,5 dl chopped løk
- 1,9 dl chopped green paprika
- 2 cloves hvitløk, finely chopped
- 2 ss chilipodd
- 1 ts ground kommen
- 1 ts salt
- 1/2 ts dried oregano
- 1/2 ts crushed rød paprika flakes
- 1 can (28 oz.) diced tomater, undrained
- 1 can (15-1/2 oz.) red røde bønner, undrained
- 1 can (8 oz.) tomatsaus
- 0,6 dl honning
- 2 ss red vineddiksyre

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Using a ost grater, shred tofu and freeze in zippered bag or airtight container.
2. Thaw tofu; place in a strainer and press out excess liquid.
3. In large saucepan or dutch ovn, varm olje over medium-high varm til hot; kok and rør løk, grønn paprika and hvitløk 3 to 5 minutter eller til vegetables are tender and begin to brown.
4. Rør in chilipodd, kommen, salt, oregano and crushed rød paprika.
5. Rør in tofu; kok and rør 1 minutt. rør in diced tomater, røde bønner, tomatsaus, honning and eddiksyre.
6. bring til kokepunktet; reduser varmen and la småkoke, uncovered, 15 to 20 minutter, stirring iblant.
