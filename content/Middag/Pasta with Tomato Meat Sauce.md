# Pasta with Tomato Meat Sauce

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg of your favorite pasta shape, uncooked
- 8 oz lean hakket storfekjøtt
- 1 medium løk, chopped
- 2 cloves hvitløk, minced
- 6 large tomater, peeled, seeded and diced
- 1/2 to 1 ts. salt
- 1/2 ts dried oregano
- 1/2 ts dried basilikum
- 1/2 ts sukker
- 0,6 dl rødvin

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Prepare pasta according to package directions.
2. While pasta is cooking, bland sammen storfekjøtt, løk and hvitløk in a large skillet; kok til meat is no longer pink. Set aside. In blender, bland sammen remaining ingredients; process for 30 seconds. tilsett tomat mixture to meat; la småkoke about 20 minutter.
3. When pasta is done, tøm well. tilsett the sauce to the pasta and servér.
