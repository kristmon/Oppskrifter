# Cheesy Baked Chicken

*These cheesy chicken legs bake up moist and juicy on the inside and tender and crunchy on the outside. Great for a casual party.*

## Ingredienser

- 0,6 dl chopped fresh parsley
- 2 ss olivenolje
- 1 clove hvitløk, crushed
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts ground black pepper
- 1,4 kg fresh kylling drumsticks
- 1,2 dl dry bread crumbs
- 0,6 dl grated Parmesan ost

## Fremgangsmåte

1. bland sammen parsley, olivenolje, hvitløk, salt and pepper in a large mixing bowl. tilsett kylling pieces, turning to coat evenly. Cover and refrigerate for 1 time.
2. Meanwhile varm ovn to 375°F.
3. bland sammen dry bread crumbs and Parmesan ost in a pie plate. Roll each kylling piece in the mixture, shaking off any excess. Arrange in a single layer in a 13x9x2-inch baking pan. bak for 1 time eller til kylling is grundig cooked.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Preparation Time**: 10 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 1 hour
- **Type**: Main Dish, Poultry
