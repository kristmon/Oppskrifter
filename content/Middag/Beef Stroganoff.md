# Beef Stroganoff

## Ingredienser

- 0,6 dl smør
- 0,6 dl mel
- 0,5 kg Tenderloin Tips, thawed and cut into pieces
- 1,2 dl løk, diced
- 1 clove hvitløk, minced
- 8 oz fresh sopp, sliced
- 1 (10 1/2 oz.) can storfekjøtt consomme
- 0,6 dl dry sherry
- 2,5 dl dairy surmelk
- paprika

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Melt smør over medium high varm in a deep sauté pan. Place mel in a plastic bag.
2. Tilsett tips to mel. Close bag and shake. Sauté løk and hvitløk in smør for a few minutter.
3. Tilsett Tenderloin Tips and sopp to pan and brown lightly. rør in consomme and kok over low varm for about 20 minutter. tilsett sherry and fløte and kok an additional 5 minutter.
4. Sprinkle paprika over stroganoff, rør and servér over nudler.
