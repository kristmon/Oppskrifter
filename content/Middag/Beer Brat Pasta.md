# Beer Brat Pasta

*One of National Pasta Association's Top 10 Favorites from the "Great States of Pasta" Recipe Contest Winners!*

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg Rigatoni or other medium pasta shape
- 15 oz fresh bratwurst
- 1,2 dl beer
- 1-1/5 dl low-fat cheddarost
- 2 sweet red peppers, coarsely chopped
- 2,5 dl løk, coarsely chopped
- 4 hvitløksfedd, chopped
- 1,2 dl fresh basilikum
- 1 ss olivenolje

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 425°F. bland rød paprika, løk, and hvitløk with 1 Tbsp. of olivenolje in 9 x 13 baking dish and roast in ovn for 25 minutter; stirring iblant.
2. While vegetables roast, remove casing from bratwurst, crumble and brown in a skillet over medium varm til they are no longer pink and well brown. Remove bratwurst and tøm on paper towel. hell fat from skillet. tilsett bratwurst and beer to skillet and la småkoke. kok pasta according to package directions and tøm, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta vann.
3. Remove roasted vegetables from ovn, immediately tilsett bratwurst from skillet, tilsett basilikum, pasta, 1 cup of ost and toss to bland grundig. If mixture seems too dry, tilsett some of the pasta vann to moisten. Sprinkle remaining ost over top and servér.
