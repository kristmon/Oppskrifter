# Skillet Chicken Stew with Corn Dumplings

*Homemade corn dumplings make this stew absolutely delicious.*

## Ingredienser

- 2 packages Stouffer's® frozen Creamed kylling, defrosted according to directions
- 1 package Stouffer's® frozen mais Soufflé
- 0,8 dl melk
- 2 ts all-purpose mel
- 2,5 dl frozen mixed vegetables
- 3 ss chopped løk
- 3/4 ts salt, divided
- 1/4 ts dried whole timian
- 1/8 ts ground black pepper
- 1,6 dl all-purpose baking bland
- 1/2 ts løk powder
- 1/8 ts ground black pepper
- 1/8 ts paprika

## Fremgangsmåte

1. bland sammen Creamed kylling, melk and mel in large skillet. tilsett mixed vegetables, 1/2 cup mais Soufflé, løk, 1/2 teaspoon salt, timian and pepper; kok, stirring iblant, over medium varm til mixture just comes to a kok. reduser varmen to low; kok, stirring iblant while preparing dumplings.
2. bland sammen baking bland, remaining mais Soufflé, 1/4 teaspoon salt, løk powder and pepper in medium bowl. For each dumpling, place about 1/4 cup batter onto stew; cover. kok for 15 to 20 minutter eller til dumplings are just firm. Sprinkle dumplings with paprika.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 5
- **Preparation Time**: 15 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 20 minutes
