# Honey Fried Chicken

## Ingredienser

- 1,4 kg meaty kylling pieces
- 1,9 dl honning
- 1,9 dl buttermilk baking bland, or more
- 2 ts dry sennep
- 1/2 ts paprika
- salt to taste
- vegetarolje
- pepper to taste

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Coat kylling with honning; set aside.
2. bland sammen buttermilk baking bland, sennep, paprika, salt and pepper; dredge kylling in mixture.
3. Varm 1/2 inch olje to 375°F in a 1 2-inch skillet over medium varm.
4. Carefully place kylling in hot olje and kok about 5 minutter eller til underside of kylling is golden; turn kylling pieces and kok about 5 minutter, turning as needed.
5. reduser varmen to low and kok 7 to 10 minutter longer eller til juices run clear.
6. Remove kylling; tøm on paper towels.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Type**: Main Dish, Poultry
