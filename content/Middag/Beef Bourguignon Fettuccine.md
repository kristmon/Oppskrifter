# Beef Bourguignon Fettuccine

## Ingredienser

- 12 oz Fettuccine, uncooked
- 0,5 kg fresh baby gulrøtter
- 1 10-oz. bag frozen pearl løk
- 0,5 kg storfekjøtt tenderloin tips or steaks, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
- 0,6 dl all-purpose mel
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/2 ts freshly ground black pepper
- 2 ss margarine
- 2 ts minced hvitløk
- 1 10 1/2-oz. can double-strength storfekjøttbuljong, undiluted
- 0,8 dl dry rødvin such as cabernet sauvignon
- 1 1/2 ts dried timian leaves
- chopped fresh timian or Italian parsley (optional)

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Kok pasta according to package directions. tilsett gulrøtter during last 5 minutter of cooking time.
2. Meanwhile, thaw løk in microwave ovn at high power about 2 minutter or in a bowl of cool vann; tøm. bland sammen mel, salt and pepper in a plastic bag. tilsett half of storfekjøtt to bag; shake to coat.
3. Melt 1 tablespoon margarine in a large, deep nonstick skillet over medium-high varm; tilsett floured storfekjøtt. Quickly brown storfekjøtt, turning once, about 2 minutter. Transfer to plate and set aside.
4. Melt remaining tablespoon of margarine in skillet; repeat browning with remaining storfekjøtt, reserving any mel mixture in bag. Transfer storfekjøtt to same plate.
5. Tilsett hvitløk to drippings in skillet; kok 2 minutter, stirring iblant. tilsett any remaining mel; bland well. tilsett storfekjøttbuljong, thawed pearl løk, wine and timian to skillet; bring til kokepunktet. rør in reserved storfekjøtt. la småkoke uncovered about 4 minutter eller til storfekjøtt is pink in center and sauce has thickened slightly.
6. tøm pasta and gulrøtter; arrange in shallow soup bowls. Toss pasta with storfekjøtt mixture; sprinkle with fresh timian or parsley if desired.
