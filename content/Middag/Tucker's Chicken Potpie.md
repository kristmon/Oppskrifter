# Tucker's Chicken Potpie

*Always an old time favorite on those cold winter nights -- also great the next day for lunch or a quick dinner.*

## Ingredienser

- 1 fresh whole kylling, cut up
- 10 dl vann
- 7,5 dl kyllingbuljong
- 3 poteter, scrubbed and diced
- 3 gulrøtter, scrubbed and sliced
- 1 løk, peeled and chopped
- 3,8 dl selleri, trimmed and sliced
- 0,6 dl smør or margarine
- 1,2 dl mel
- 5 dl half-and-half
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/2 ts ground black pepper
- 2 9-inch pie crusts

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a large soup or pasta pot over medium varm, kok kylling in vann til mør, 45 minutt to 1 time; tøm, rinse, cool and debone.
2. In a saucepan, varm the kyllingbuljong and kok poteter, gulrøtter, løk and selleri til mør, 15 to 20 minutter; tøm, reserving broth.
3. forvarm ovn to 400°F. Grease a 13x9x2-inch baking dish; set aside.
4. In a skillet, melt smør. tilsett mel, stirring til smooth. kok for 1 minutt, stirring konstant. Gradually rør in reserved broth, half-and-half, salt and pepper. kok over medium varm, stirring iblant, til thickened and bubbly.
5. Tilsett the vegetables and kylling, stirring to coat, and hell into prepared 13x9x2-inch baking dish. Roll out the pie crusts and place on top, overlapping slightly in the center. bak 30 to 35 minutter eller til gyllen brown.
