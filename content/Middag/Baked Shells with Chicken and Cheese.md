# Baked Shells with Chicken and Cheese

*Pasta shells are smothered and swirled around succulent chicken bites and flavored with parsley and garlic.*

## Ingredienser

- 8 medium ounces pasta shells
- 2,5 dl 2% melk
- 1/2 ts hvitløk salt
- 1/8 ts ground black pepper
- 5 dl shredded Monterey Jack ost, divided
- 5 dl chopped cooked kylling meat
- 2 ts crushed dried parsley

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Kok pasta as per package instructions; tøm and set aside.
2. forvarm ovn to 375°F.
3. In a saucepan over medium varm, bring melk to just boiling eller til small bubbles appear around the edges, about 5 minutter. tilsett hvitløk salt, pepper, and about 3/4 of the shredded ost; whisk til melted.
4. bland sammen sauce with kylling, parsley and kokt pasta in a baking dish. Top with remaining ost. bak for 20 minutter eller til bubbling and browned on top.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Preparation Time**: 10 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 35 minutes
- **Type**: Main Dish, Poultry
