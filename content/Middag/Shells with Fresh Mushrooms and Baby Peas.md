# Shells with Fresh Mushrooms and Baby Peas

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg fresh white sopp
- 3 ss extra virgin olivenolje
- 2,5 dl sliced purreløk or scallions (green løk)
- 2,5 dl canned vegetable or low-sodium kyllingbuljong
- 1 can (14-1/2 ounces) diced tomater, drained
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts ground black pepper
- 3,8 dl frozen baby peas
- 0,5 kg pasta shells
- 1,2 dl freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano ost

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Trim sopp and thickly skjær i skiver (makes about 6 cups); set aside. In a large pot, bring 6 quarts of salted vann to a kok. In a large skillet, varm olivenolje over medium-high varm.
2. Tilsett purreløk and prepared sopp; kok stirring til mør, about 5 minutter. rør in broth, tomater, salt and pepper; kok and rør, uncovered, til the liquid is reduced by half, about 5 minutter; rør in peas during the last 2 minutter.
3. Meanwhile, kok pasta in the boiling vann according to package directions til al dente. Transfer hot pasta to a large serving bowl; tilsett sopp and pea mixture and the ost; toss til pasta is coated.
