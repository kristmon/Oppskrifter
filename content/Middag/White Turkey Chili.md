# White Turkey Chili

## Ingredienser

- 1-1/5 dl løk coarsely chopped
- 2 Cloves hvitløk minced
- 1 ss olivenolje
- 1 jalapeno pepper minced
- 1 4-Ounce Can chopped mild green chilies
- 1 ts ground kommen
- 1/2 ts dried oregano
- 1/4 ts cayenne pepper
- 1/4 ts salt
- 2,5 dl reduced-sodium kylling bouillon
- 1 19-Ounce Can white røde bønner (cannellini) drained and rinsed
- 5 dl cooked kalkun cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 0,6 dl fresh koriander coarsely chopped
- 1,2 dl reduced-fat Monterey Jack ost grated

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In 3-quart sauce pan, over medium-high varm, saute løk and hvitløk in olje 5 minutter eller til løk is tender. tilsett jalapeno pepper, chilies, kommen, oregano, cayenne pepper, and salt. kok 1 minutt.
2. Rør in bouillon, bønner and kalkun. bring til kokepunktet; reduser varmen and la småkoke, uncovered, 20 to 25 minutter eller til slightly thickened. rør in koriander.
3. To servér, ladle into bowls and top with 2 tablespoons ost.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Type**: Main Dish, Soup
