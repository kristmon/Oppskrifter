# Rock Cornish Game Hens With Prune Stuffing

## Ingredienser

- 18 pitted prunes
- Dry sherry, to cover prunes
- 6 cornish hens (1 lb each or 3 two-pound chickens)
- 1 Tbs canola oil
- Salt, to taste
- Freshly-ground black pepper, to taste
- 2 Tbs currant jelly
- 2 Tbs sherry

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Place prunes in bowl. Marinate in sherry overnight.
2. Preheat oven to 425 degrees.
3. Wash hens. Season cavities with salt and pepper. Drain prunes. Stuff 3 prunes in 1-pound hens or 6 prunes in 2-pound hens. Close with toothpicks. Tie legs together. Place in baking dish on rack. Brush with oil, salt, and pepper, and place in oven. Bake 25 minutes.
4. Melt jelly with 2 tablespoons sherry. Brush hens with glaze and bake 10 minutes. Repeat. Bake 45 minutes in all. If using 2-pound hens, cut in half down the middle. Arrange hens with 3 prunes under each half. Remove toothpicks and untie legs.
