# Spaghetti & Chicken in Roasted Garlic Sauce

*Cooked with a minimum of oil, this creamy pasta dish is enriched with chicken stock, evaporated milk and nonfat sour cream. Roasted garlic and sage add depth to the sauce.*

## Ingredienser

- 6 large clove hvitløk
- 1 ss kyllingbuljong
- 8 oz spaghetti pasta
- 4 oz Nestlé® Carnation® Evaporated melk
- 2 ss cornstarch
- 0,5 kg fresh boneless, skinless kylling breasts, cut into 1/2-inch kutt i terninger
- 3,8 dl kyllingbuljong, divided
- 2,5 dl sliced sopp
- 2 ss thinly sliced green løk
- 1/2 ts crushed rød paprika flakes
- 1 ss chopped fresh sage
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts ground black pepper
- 3 ss nonfat surmelk

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 350°F.
2. Place unpeeled hvitløksfedd on a piece of aluminum foil. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon kylling stock and wrap tightly. bak in ovn til mør, about 45 minutter. When cool enough to handle, squeeze pulp from cloves, mash and set aside.
3. When hvitløk is done, kok spaghetti according to package directions; tøm.
4. Dissolve cornstarch in evaporated melk in a small bowl and set aside.
5. Varm a large, nonstick skillet over medium-high varm. Spray with vegetable cooking spray and sauté kylling til gyllen brown, about 5 minutter; transfer to a plate. tilsett 1/2 cup kyllingbuljong, sopp, green løk and rød paprika flakes and la småkoke over high varm til liquid evaporates, about 7 minutter. Return kylling to skillet along with remaining kyllingbuljong, roasted hvitløk, sage, salt and pepper; bring til kokepunktet, reduser varmen and la småkoke for 5 to 7 more minutter. rør in reserved cornstarch mixture, la småkoke til slightly thickened, about 3 minutter. Whisk in surmelk.
6. Toss with spaghetti and servér immediately.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Preparation Time**: 20 minutes
- **Cooking Time**: 1 hour and 5 minutes
- **Type**: Main Dish, Pasta, Poultry
