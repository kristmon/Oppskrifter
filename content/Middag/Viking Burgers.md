# Viking Burgers

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket kalkun
- 1-2 ss prepared horseradish
- 1-1/2 ts paprika
- 1-1/2 ts Dijon-style sennep
- 1/4 ts pepper
- dash salt
- 4 Hamburger buns toasted

## Fremgangsmåte

1. bland sammen all ingredients. Shape into 4 burgers about 1/2-inch thick.
2. Place burgers on lightly greased broiling pan. Broil burgers 6-inches from varm, 3 to 4 minutter per side til no longer pink.
3. servér on toasted hamburger buns with additional sennep, if desired.

## Info
