# "Red Cooked" Pork & Vegetables

## Ingredienser

- 1 kg boneless svinekjøtt shoulder (Boston butt), cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes
- 2 ss vegetarolje
- 2 ts minced ingefær root
- 1,6 dl Kikkoman rør-stekk sauce
- 2 ss dry sherry
- 1 ts fennel seed, crushed
- 3 gulrøtter, cut into 1-inch lengths
- 1/1 kg fresh sopp, quartered
- 1 bunch green løk, cut into 1-inch lengths, separate whites from tops
- 2 ts cornstarch
- Hot kokt ris

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Tilsett rør-stekk sauce, sherry, fennel and 3/4 cup vann to pan; bring til kokepunktet. reduser varmen and la småkoke, covered, 45 minutter stirring iblant.
2. Tilsett gulrøtter; la småkoke, covered, 30 minutter.
3. Tilsett sopp and white parts of green løk. la småkoke, covered, 10 minutter, eller til svinekjøtt and vegetables are tender. rør in green løk tops; cover and kok 3 minutter longer.
4. Meanwhile, bland cornstarch and 2 tablespoons vann. rør into meat mixture; kok, stirring, 1 minutter, eller til sauce boils and thickens slightly.
