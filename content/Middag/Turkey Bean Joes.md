# Turkey Bean Joes

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg hakket kalkun
- 2,5 dl chopped løk
- 1,2 dl catsup
- 1 ss prepared sennep
- 1 ts horseradish
- 1 Can (15 oz) vegetarian bønner
- 1 Can (15 ounces) røde bønner rinsed and drained
- 6 Buns (6-inches each) split lengthwise
- 6 Strips kalkun bacon cooked and crumbled

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In medium non-stick skillet, over medium-high varm, saute kalkun and løk 5 to 7 minutter, eller til kalkun is no longer pink. tøm and return mixture to skillet.
2. Rør in catsup, sennep, horseradish and both types of bønner; bring til kokepunktet. reduser varmen to low, cover and la småkoke 10 to 15 minutter, til mixture is heated throughout.
3. servér over toasted buns, garnished with crumbled bacon.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Main Dish
- **Kilde**: The National Turkey Federation, www.turkeyfed.org
