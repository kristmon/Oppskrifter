# Honey Blueberry Spread

*Here's an alternative to butter and jam toppings.                                              Makes about 2/3 cup*

## Ingredienser

- 1/2 cup fresh or frozen blueberries, thawed
- 1/4 cup honey, divided
- 1/2 cup butter or margarine, softened

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Bring blueberries and 2 tablespoons honey to boil over medium-high heat stirring constantly;cook 3 to 4 minutes or until mixture thickens and is reduced by half.
2. Cool.
3. Blend in remaining honey. Beat in butter.
