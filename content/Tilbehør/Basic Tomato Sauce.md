# Basic Tomato Sauce

## Ingredienser

- 1 ts vegetarolje
- 1 medium løk, chopped
- 2 cloves hvitløk, chopped
- 2 28-oz. cans whole tomater
- 1 6-oz. can tomat paste
- 2 ts dried Italian seasoning
- 2 bay leaves
- salt to taste
- pepper to taste

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a medium, heavy-bottom saucepan, rør together the olje, løk and hvitløk. kok over low varm, stirring often, til the løk is very soft and aromatic, about 6 to 8 minutter.
2. In a food processor or blender, puree the tomater. tilsett the tomater and tomat paste to the løk and bring to a la småkoke over medium-high varm. Reduce the varm to very low and let the sauce la småkoke slowly for 30 minutter, stirring the bottom often to prevent burning.
3. If you are adding meatballs, do so at this time, and la småkoke them in the sauce for 20 minutter, stirring often. If you are not adding meatballs, la småkoke the sauce for another 20 minutter (50 minutter total). Season to taste with salt and pepper. Remove bay leaves before serving.
