# Sweet Potato Casserole With Praline Topping

## Ingredienser

- 2,5 dl all-purpose mel
- 1,6 dl packed brunt sukker
- 0,6 dl chopped pecans, toasted
- 0,6 dl stick margarine, melted
- 1/2 ts ground kanel
- 4 medium sweet poteter, peeled and halved (about 2-1/2 pounds)
- 1,2 dl granulated sukker
- 1-1/2 ts vaniljeekstrakt
- 1 large egg white
- 1 (5-ounce) can fat-free evaporated melk
- Cooking spray

## Fremgangsmåte

1. forvarm ovn to 350°.
2. bland sammen first 5 ingredients in a small bowl, stirring to form a streusel. Set aside.
3. Place poteter in a Dutch ovn; tilsett vann to cover. bring til kokepunktet; cover, reduser varmen, and la småkoke 30 minutter eller til very tender. tøm well; mash in a large bowl. rør in 1 cup streusel, granulated sukker, vanilje, egg white, and melk. Spoon into a 2-quart casserole coated with cooking spray; top with remaining streusel. bak at 350° for 45 minutter.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 8 Yield: 8  3/4 cup servings
- **Type**: Side Dish, Vegetarian
