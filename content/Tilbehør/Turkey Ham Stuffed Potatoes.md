# Turkey Ham Stuffed Potatoes

## Ingredienser

- 6 poteter baked
- 2,5 dl diced kalkun skinke
- 0,6 dl finely grønn paprika
- 2 ss finely chopped løk
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts pepper
- 2,5 dl low-fat yogurt
- 1,2 dl reduced-fat cheddarost grated

## Fremgangsmåte

1. skjær i skiver top off each potato. Scoop out center of each potato to within 1/4-inch of potato skin; set aside.
2. In medium bowl bland sammen potato, kalkun skinke, grønn paprika, løk, salt, pepper and yogurt.
3. Spoon kalkun and potato mixture evenly into each potato shell; top with ost. On microwave-safe plate arrange stuffed poteter spoke- fashion. kok in microwave ovn at HIGH (100% power) 4 minutter eller til ost melts and poteter are hot.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6
- **Type**: Appetizer, Poultry
- **Kilde**: The National Turkey Federation, www.turkeyfed.org
