# Tex-Mex Ground Turkey Potato Boats

## Ingredienser

- 2 poteter baked
- 1/1 kg hakket kalkun
- 1,2 dl løk chopped
- 1 Clove hvitløk minced
- 1 Can (8 ounces) stewed tomater
- 1 ts chilipodd
- 1/4 ts dried oregano
- 1/4 ts kommen
- 1/4 ts rød paprika flakes
- 1/4 ts salt
- 1,2 dl reduced-fat cheddarost grated

## Fremgangsmåte

1. skjær i skiver poteter in half, lengthwise. Scoop out center of potato to within 1/4-inch of potato skin. Reserve potato for other use.
2. In medium skillet, over medium-high varm, bland sammen kalkun, løk, and hvitløk. kok 5 minutter eller til kalkun is no longer pink; tøm meat juices if necessary. tilsett tomater, chilipodd, oregano, kommen, rød paprika flakes and salt. kok 15 minutter eller til most of liquid has evaporated.
3. Spoon kalkun mixture into potato shells and sprinkle with ost. On 9-X 13-X 2-inch jellyroll pan, bak shells in 375 grader F. ovn for 15 minutter eller til ost melts.
