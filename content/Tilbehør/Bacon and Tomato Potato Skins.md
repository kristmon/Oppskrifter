# Bacon and Tomato Potato Skins

## Ingredienser

- 6 large baking poteter
- 2 ts cooking olje
- 1 ts chilipodd
- Several dashes bottled hot pepper sauce
- 1,6 dl chopped Canadian-style bacon or chopped, cooked kalkun bacon
- 1 medium tomat, finely chopped
- 2 ss finely chopped green løk
- 4 oz cheddarost or reduced-fat cheddarost, shredded (2,5 dl)
- 1,2 dl dairy surmelk (optional)

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Scrub poteter grundig and prick with a fork. Arrange on a microwave-safe plate. Micro-kok, uncovered, on 100% power (high) for 17 to 22 minutter or till almost tender, rearranging once. (Or, bak poteter in a 425 grader F. ovn for 40 to 45 minutter or till tender.) Cool.
2. Halve each potato lengthwise. Scoop out the inside of each potato half, leaving about a 1/4-inch-thick shell. Cover and chill the leftover fluffy white part of poteter for another use.
3. bland sammen the cooking olje, chilipodd, and hot pepper sauce. With a pastry brush, brush the insides of the potato halves with the olje mixture. Cut the potato halves in half lengthwise. Return to the baking sheet. Sprinkle potato quarters with bacon, tomat, and green løk. Top with ost. To make ahead, cover and chill for up to 24 timer.
4. bak in 450 grader F. ovn for 10 to 12 minutter or till ost is melted and potato quarters are heated through. servér with surmelk, if desired.
