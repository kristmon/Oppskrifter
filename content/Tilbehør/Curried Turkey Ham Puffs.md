# Curried Turkey Ham Puffs

## Ingredienser

**Filling**
- 1/1 kg kalkun skinke finely chopped
- 0,6 dl løk finely chopped
- 0,6 dl selleri finely chopped
- 0,6 dl unsweetened applesauce
- 2 ss no cholesterol reduced-calorie majones
- 3/4 ts curry powder
**Pastry**
- 1,2 dl vann
- 0,6 dl margarine at room temperature
- 1,2 dl mel
- 2 egg at room temperature

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In medium-size bowl bland sammen skinke, løk, selleri, applesauce, majones and curry powder.
2. Use as a bred for sandwiches or as a filling for pastry puffs (recipe follows). Spoon 1 tablespoon kalkun filling on bottom half of each pastry puff. Replace top half on puff.
3. Place one ovn rack in upper third of ovn and the other rack in the lower third of the ovn.
4. In medium-size saucepan, over medium-high varm, bring vann and margarine to kok. ta av varmen; tilsett mel. Beat with spoon til smooth. reduser varmen to low; return pan to varm. Continue cooking and stirring mixture til it forms a ball (about 1 to 2 minutter). ta av varmen and allow mixture to cool 2 minutter. Using a spoon, tilsett egg, one at a time, beating vigorously, til dough is smooth.
5. On 2 ungreased cookie sheets, drop 24 rounded teaspoons of dough, 1-inch apart. bak puffs at 425 grader F. 15 minutter. reduser varmen to 375 grader F. and continue baking puffs 15 minutter. Do not open door during baking and do not remove puffs from ovn til they are firm to touch. Allow puffs to cool completely. Cut puffs in half crosswise, and fill.
