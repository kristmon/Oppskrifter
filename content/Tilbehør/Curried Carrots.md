# Curried Carrots

## Ingredienser

- 0,5 kg gulrøtter, peeled and cut into 1/2 inch slices
- 1 ss apricot jam
- 1-1/2 ts lemon juice
- 3/4 ts Dijon sennep
- 1 ts curry powder
- 1 ts unsalted smør
- 2 ts vegetarolje
- 1-1/2 ts brunt sukker
- 0,6 dl raisins

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Place gulrøtter in a steamer basket over boiling vann. Cover saucepan and steam 5-7 minutter eller til almost tender.
2. Remove gulrøtter from steamer and set aside. bland sammen apricot bred, lemon juice, sennep and curry powder in a small bowl. varm smør and olje in a heavy nonstick skillet over medium high varm. When olje is hot sauté gulrøtter 1 minutt. rør in brunt sukker and raisins and sauté another 1-2 minutter. tilsett spice mixture, stirring konstant 2-3 minutter, scraping down sides of skillet, eller til gulrøtter are glazed.

## Info

- **Preparation Time**: 10 minutes
