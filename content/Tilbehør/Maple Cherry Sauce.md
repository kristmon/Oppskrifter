# Maple Cherry Sauce

*Cherries team up with maple syrup for a flavorful sauce that's terrific with roasted or grilled meats or poultry.*

## Ingredienser

- 0,8 dl cherry juice bland
- 2 ss cornstarch
- 2,5 dl frozen unsweetened tart cherries, thawed and well drained
- 1,9 dl real maple or maple-flavored syrup
- 1,2 dl chopped walnuts
- 1 ts grated orange peel

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Put cherry juice bland and cornstarch in a small saucepan; bland well. kok over medium varm til thickened. tilsett cherries, maple-flavored syrup, walnuts and orange peel; bland well.
2. kok, stirring frequently, over low varm til all ingredients are hot.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 6 Yield: 1 1/2 cups
- **Type**: Side Dish
- **Kilde**: Cherry Marketing Institute, www.cherrymkt.org
