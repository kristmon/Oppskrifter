# Turkey Nachos

## Ingredienser

- 7,5 dl no-salt-added tortilla chips
- 2 Slices (1 ounce each) kalkun skinke cut into 1/2-inch kutt i terninger
- 0,6 dl cheddarost shredded
- 1 ss canned green chilies chopped
- 1 ss salsa

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Line toaster ovn tray with aluminum foil. Arrange chips on tray. Sprinkle with skinke, ost, chilies and salsa.
2. In toaster ovn at 350 grader F., varm nachos 5 to 8 minutter eller til ost melts.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 1
- **Type**: Appetizer, Poultry
- **Kilde**: The National Turkey Federation, www.turkeyfed.org
