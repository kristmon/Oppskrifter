# Provencal Mushroom and White Bean Stew

## Ingredienser

- 2 ss olivenolje
- 0,5 kg fresh white sopp, sliced (about 5 cups)
- 2,5 dl chopped løk
- 1 ts minced hvitløk
- 3/4 ts dried timian, crushed
- 2 cans (13-3/4 oz each) ready-to-servér kyllingbuljong
- 1 can (14-1/2 oz) stewed tomater, cut into bite size pieces
- 0,6 dl dry hvitvini
- 2 cans (15 ozs each) white (cannellini) bønner, drained

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a Dutch ovn or large saucepot, varm olje til hot. tilsett sopp, løk, hvitløk and timian; kok and rør til løk is very tender and sopp are slightly golden, about 7 minutter.
2. Tilsett kyllingbuljong, tomater with their liquid and wine; bring til kokepunktet; cover and la småkoke to bland flavors, about 15 minutter
3. In a small bowl, mash 1 cup of the bønner til smooth; tilsett to stew.
4. Rør in remaining bønner, varm til hot. servér immediately with a mound of steamed ris, if desired.

## Info

- **Porsjoner**: 4
- **Type**: Appetizer, Soup
