# Mushroom Chili

## Ingredienser

- 2 ss vegetarolje
- 2,5 dl chopped løk
- 1 ss minced hvitløk
- 2 ss chilipodd
- 1 ts ground kommen
- 1 1/1 kg fresh white sopp, sliced (about 9 cups)
- 8 oz fresh shiitake sopp (stems removed), sliced (about 2-1/5 dl)
- 1 can (about 14-1/2 ounces) stewed tomater
- 1 can (about 19 ounces) white røde bønner, rinsed and drained
- 1,2 dl sliced ripe olives

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a large saucepan varm olje til hot. tilsett løk and hvitløk. kok, stirring frequently, til løk is tender, about 5 minutter.
2. Rør in chilipodd and kommen; kok til fragrant about 30 seconds. tilsett white and shiitake sopp; kok, stirring iblant, til sopp are tender, 6 to 8 minutter.
3. Tilsett tomater, bønner, olives and 1/2 cup vann. la småkoke uncovered, to bland flavors, about 10 minutter
