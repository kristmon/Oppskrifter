# Tortilla Pinwheels

## Ingredienser

- 1 Tbs fat-free cream cheese
- 2 Tbs shredded reduced-fat Cheddar cheese
- 2 Tbs mild salsa
- 1 Tbs chopped green onion tops
- 1/8 tsp chili powder
- 1 low-fat four tortilla - (6" dia)

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In a small bowl, stir together the cream cheese and Cheddar cheese with a fork until the Cheddar is incorporated. Stir in the salsa, green onion, and chili powder.
2. Spread the mixture evenly on the tortilla. Roll up and cut off the two ends. Cut tortilla into 4 pieces and serve.
