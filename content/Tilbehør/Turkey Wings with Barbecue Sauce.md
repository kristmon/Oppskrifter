# Turkey Wings with Barbecue Sauce

## Ingredienser

- 2 Large cloves hvitløk minced
- 2,5 dl chili sauce or ketchup
- 0,6 dl vegetarolje
- 0,8 dl cider eddiksyre
- 0,6 dl løk chopped
- 1-1/2 ss worcestershiresaus
- 1/2 ts salt
- 1/4 ts freshly ground pepper
- 1/2 ts dried timian
- 4 Each kalkun wings about 3-3/4 pounds

## Fremgangsmåte

1. In small saucepan, bland sammen all ingredients, except kalkun. la småkoke, uncovered, over low varm for 15 to 20 minutter. Arrange kalkun wings in foil-lined roasting pan. hell grillsaus over kalkun; cover and marinate for 2 timer in refrigerator. Turn wings iblant in sauce. Remove from refrigerator.
2. To Grill: Spray grill with vegetarolje. Grill wings til browned, about 7 minutter on each side; turn and baste frequently; continue to kok about 1 time til cooked throughout and tender.
3. To bak: bak loosely covered at 325 grader F. for 1-1/2 timer. Baste frequently. Uncover; continue roasting for 30 minutter eller til kalkun is lightly browned, tender and cooked through.
